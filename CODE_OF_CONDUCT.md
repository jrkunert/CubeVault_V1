# CubeVault Code of Conduct

## Purpose

CubeVault is committed to fostering a professional, respectful, and collaborative engineering environment.

This Code of Conduct defines the expected standards of behavior for everyone participating in the project.

---

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to:

- Treat everyone with professionalism and respect.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Focus discussions on technical merit and project goals.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Help maintain a positive engineering culture.
- Respect project governance and established processes.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or insulting language.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Deliberate disruption of project activities.
- Sharing confidential or proprietary information without authorization.
- Misrepresentation of work or authorship.
- Knowingly submitting malicious or harmful code.

---

## Engineering Conduct

Contributors should:

- Follow the CubeVault Constitution.
- Follow the Engineering Standards.
- Respect architectural decisions.
- Keep documentation current.
- Produce maintainable, production-quality work.
- Raise concerns through appropriate project discussions.

---

## AI-Assisted Development

AI-assisted development is supported within CubeVault.

All AI-generated work must:

- Be reviewed by a human contributor.
- Comply with project governance.
- Follow architectural standards.
- Meet the same quality expectations as manually written work.

Responsibility for submitted work always remains with the contributor.

---

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns regarding project conduct should be reported to the project maintainer or designated project lead.

Reports will be handled professionally and confidentially where appropriate.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to:

- Request revisions to contributions.
- Remove inappropriate content.
- Restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.
- Take any action necessary to maintain a productive project environment.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- Repository discussions
- Pull requests
- Issue tracking
- Documentation
- Code reviews
- Project meetings
- Other official CubeVault collaboration spaces

---

Thank you for helping maintain a professional and collaborative engineering community.
